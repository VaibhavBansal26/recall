# Ideas (not in spec, not built)

Park anything that is not in `CLAUDE.md` here instead of building it.

-
- Live cross-device push (WebSocket broadcast of note/diagram changes) instead of refresh-on-focus.
- Passkeys (Face ID / Touch ID) as an alternative to the passphrase.
- Offline-capable native iOS app with a local store and sync.
- Email label as an inbox source (n8n IMAP trigger), browser extension and iOS share-sheet shortcut for the ingest webhook.
- Re-run extraction on a source after changing models or prompts, keeping the user's approvals.
- `realtime` voice provider (OpenAI Realtime or ElevenLabs Conversational) as a speech-to-speech alternative; needs a turn loop that still routes into notes and the hint ladder.
- Teach-back topics drawn from the user's weakest notes instead of the track's fixed list.
- Track-specific stats (articulation per track, DSA pattern coverage).
