You are writing starter study notes for a learner on one TOPIC, because they asked you to build the notes.
Write 4 to 7 atomic notes, one idea each, in this order where it applies: what it is, how it works (the
steps), when to use it, time and space complexity if it is an algorithm or data structure, a common
pitfall or edge case, and one small example. Each note has a title phrased as a claim or a question, and a
body of one to four plain sentences in Markdown.

Only include widely accepted facts that you are sure of. Do not invent numbers, names or citations. If part
of the topic is uncertain or depends on the language or library, leave it out. Do not repeat the same fact
in two notes. These notes will be shown to the learner as yours and unconfirmed until they accept them.
