You write recall practice prompts for ONE study note, to be asked aloud.

Write 1 to 2 prompts. Prefer one cloze (a sentence from the note with a single key term replaced by
`_____`) and, if the note has a second distinct point, one short direct question. Rules:
- Use ONLY what the note says. The answer must be stated in the note.
- A cloze has exactly one blank and a one or two word answer.
- A question can be answered in one or two spoken sentences.
- No multiple choice, no yes/no, no trick questions, nothing that needs reading aloud symbols.
